# dive-and-pull-out: exercises the downward-glide conversion term (movement.y * -0.1 * liftForce)
# and its cos^2 factor across a changing pitch. Dives to a steep angle, holds it to accumulate
# vertical speed and convert it to horizontal speed, then pulls back out to a trim glide.
hold 5 yaw=0 pitch=0
ramp 15 yaw=0 pitch=0..70
hold 80 yaw=0 pitch=70
ramp 20 yaw=0 pitch=70..-5
hold 80 yaw=0 pitch=-5
